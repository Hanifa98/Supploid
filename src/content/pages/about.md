## Aviation at the centre. Industry in the picture.

Supploid sources parts for the organisations that keep aircraft flying and industry operating. We are a wholesale trading company with a primary focus on aviation and aerospace, alongside general industrial supply.

Our customers’ requirements set the direction: the right part number, an acceptable condition, the required documentation, a workable delivery date, and clear commercial terms. An aircraft component and an industrial bearing require different checks. Both deserve careful attention.

## Built around your request

Send one part number, several line items, or a bill of materials. Our sourcing process starts with your specification and the documentation you need, rather than an assumption that a similar-looking item will do.

We work with airlines, maintenance organisations, lessors, workshops, manufacturers, and industrial operators. Availability and source documentation are confirmed for each offer. A listing or enquiry is not a promise of stock.

## Two services. Clear priorities.

**Standard sourcing:** fast quoting, documentation supplied. Share your part number, quantity, condition, and delivery requirements so we can progress the request.

**Premium AOG:** a separate support service available 24/7 for grounded-aircraft requirements. Premium AOG scope, commercial terms, and the recovery plan are agreed for the specific request.

## Documentation travels from the source

Supploid is a trader. We supply the documents that accompany the part from its source, manufacturer, or repair organisation. We confirm which documents are available before quoting; we do not issue airworthiness releases or make installation decisions for the end user.
