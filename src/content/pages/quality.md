## Requirements first

Part identification, condition, configuration, quantity, and documentation requirements belong in the RFQ. We confirm available source documentation before quoting, including any gaps that affect your purchasing decision.

A sourcing offer is subject to availability and the agreed terms. It does not replace the buyer’s technical review or the installer’s determination of eligibility.

## Documentation supplied from the source

For aviation enquiries, specify whether you need an FAA 8130-3, EASA Form 1, Certificate of Conformance, trace to source, non-incident statement, or dual release. These are supplied with the part where available from its source. Availability depends on the specific item, its history, the originator, and the intended transaction.

For industrial enquiries, specify manufacturer CofC, EN 10204 2.1 or 3.1 documentation, and any relevant RoHS or REACH declarations. These are supplied with the part where available. Requesting a document does not mean it exists for every product.

Supploid does not issue airworthiness documents. The source, manufacturer, or repair organisation remains the originator of the accompanying documents. We do not describe a document as a substitute for the underlying part history.

## Discrepancies and suspect parts

Our proposed supply process checks consistency between the quoted identity, declared condition, source paperwork, and buyer requirements. Missing identifiers, conflicting serial numbers, unexplained alterations, or incomplete trace need clarification before the transaction proceeds.

If a discrepancy or counterfeit concern arises, stop acceptance and installation, preserve the packaging and paperwork, and notify us with the purchase reference and evidence. The matter must be investigated with the source and the buyer’s quality function. Do not treat a commercial explanation as a technical release.

## Inspection and acceptance

Tell us about incoming-inspection criteria before placing an order. Inspection arrangements, the acceptance period, and any independent review must be agreed in the written offer. We do not publish an unsupported universal inspection guarantee or acceptance window.

On receipt, compare the delivered item and accompanying records with the agreed order. Where a discrepancy is found, retain identifying evidence and contact [info@supploid.com](mailto:info@supploid.com) before return shipment.

## Returns

Return eligibility, notification periods, and any restocking or transport charges are governed by the agreed written sale terms. Request return instructions before shipping a part back. This page does not promise a fixed warranty or unconditional return right.

## Destination and end use

We ask for end use and end-user country to support applicable trade-control screening. Depending on the transaction, EU dual-use controls under Regulation (EU) 2021/821 and US EAR or ITAR requirements may be relevant. The part, source, destination, end user, and intended use determine the checks and any authorisation needed.

Supplying a destination does not itself clear a transaction for export. Availability, technical eligibility, and lawful shipment are separate questions.

References: [EU dual-use framework](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2021/821/oj/eng), [US Bureau of Industry and Security](https://www.bis.gov/), [US Directorate of Defense Trade Controls](https://www.pmddtc.state.gov/).
