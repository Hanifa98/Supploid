> This notice is a draft. Online collection is not enabled in the default build. The controller’s legal identity, retention policy, processing basis, and supplier arrangements must be confirmed before online forms are activated.

## Contact

For questions about your information, contact Supploid at [info@supploid.com](mailto:info@supploid.com). The legal controller information for the final notice remains to be confirmed.

## Information in a request

The standard quote form asks for your first and last name, email, part number or industrial description, quantity, and message, with optional phone and company name. The premium AOG form asks for a callback number, part number, aircraft type, and email address. Please provide business information relevant to the enquiry and avoid unnecessary personal or confidential material.

The intended purposes are to prepare and discuss a quote, manage an urgent sourcing request, communicate about supply, and perform relevant trade-control checks. Information is not collected through these forms for marketing subscriptions.

## Processing basis

The final notice must identify the appropriate basis for each processing purpose, which may include steps requested before a contract, legitimate business interests, or applicable legal obligations. A checkbox acknowledging this notice does not remove rights or make consent an appropriate basis for every purpose.

## Service providers

The proposed online flow uses Cloudflare to serve the site and protect forms, and Resend to deliver the enquiry and confirmation emails. Business information submitted in the form is included in those emails. The forms do not accept file uploads.

Rate-limit protection stores short-lived submission timestamps under a hashed network identifier. It does not store quote contents. Provider roles, agreements, international transfers, and applicable safeguards must be verified in the final notice.

## Your rights

Depending on the processing and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, or to object. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. Requests can be sent to the contact email above. You may also complain to a competent supervisory authority, as applicable to your request.

## Analytics and storage

The default build contains no analytics tracker. If enabled, the intended analytics service is Cloudflare Web Analytics. The site does not use advertising pixels or marketing cookies. Your explicit light/dark theme preference is stored in your browser’s local storage; no form details are stored there. Form protection is separate from audience measurement and is provided by Cloudflare Turnstile when configured.
