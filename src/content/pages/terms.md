> This is a draft commercial framework, not final adopted terms of sale. The seller’s legal identity, payment terms, warranties, liability provisions, and governing-law terms require confirmation before launch. The final written agreement must identify the contracting parties.

## Enquiries and quotations

Submitting an RFQ requests an offer. It does not place an order, reserve stock, or commit either party to a transaction. Fast quoting, documentation supplied. Availability and delivery timing are confirmed in the offer.

Each offer should identify the part, quantity, condition, source documentation, currency, price, quote validity, and lead time. Alternatives require the buyer’s explicit agreement.

## Orders and payment

Order acceptance, payment arrangements, title, and any deposit must be stated in the final written agreement. Do not assume credit terms, exchange terms, or a fixed order-cancellation right from this website.

## Documentation and technical decisions

Accompanying documents originate with the source, manufacturer, or repair organisation. Available documentation is confirmed before quoting and supplied with the part as agreed. Supploid does not issue airworthiness releases. The buyer and installer retain responsibility for their technical and installation decisions.

## Delivery

Confirm the ship-to location, consignee, delivery schedule, transport arrangements, and agreed Incoterm in the quote. Availability is subject to confirmation. Freight, duties, taxes, insurance, and customs responsibilities must be identified in the agreement.

## Inspection, returns, and discrepancies

Agree incoming-inspection criteria, acceptance timing, any warranty, and return procedures before ordering. Report discrepancies with identifying evidence and request instructions before sending goods back. This draft does not create an unconfirmed return window or warranty period.

## Premium AOG

Premium AOG is a separate support service available 24/7. Its scope, commercial terms, and any time-sensitive sourcing or shipping arrangements are agreed for the individual request.

## Trade controls

Orders are subject to applicable export controls, sanctions, end-use restrictions, and any required authorisations. Requested end-use information must be complete and accurate. A quote does not constitute export clearance.
