## Match the full specification

An industrial bearing, fastener, or seal often carries important detail beyond its basic dimensions. Provide the manufacturer part number, standard, material, finish, fit, and application requirements that matter to your equipment.

Identify whether an exact brand is required or alternatives may be considered. Alternative proposals need your technical review; similarity in dimensions does not establish suitability.

## Documentation belongs in the RFQ

Manufacturer CofC, EN 10204 material documentation, and relevant RoHS or REACH declarations are supplied with the part where available. Tell us which documents are mandatory and whether batch or heat-number traceability is required.

We confirm available source documentation before quoting. Specific inspection documents cannot be assumed to exist for every standard catalogue component.
