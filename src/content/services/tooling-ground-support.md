## Define the task as well as the tool

Share the tooling reference, equipment model, aircraft application, and the task it supports. Specify interfaces, capacity, power supply, and any required accessories. Those details help avoid an offer that looks correct but does not fit the job.

## Calibration and supporting records

If calibration records, manuals, inspection records, or manufacturer documentation are needed, list them in the enquiry. We confirm what the source can supply before quoting. Calibration status and remaining validity must be assessed for the offered item and your intended use.

## Plan the physical movement

Ground support equipment can require different packing, handling, and freight arrangements from a boxed component. Include the destination, any site-access constraints, and the required delivery date. Commercial terms and logistics are agreed in the offer.
