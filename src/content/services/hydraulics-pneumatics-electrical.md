## Configuration makes the difference

For hydraulic and pneumatic components, specify operating pressure, media, connection type, seals, and relevant ratings. For electrical items, include voltage, current, connection, enclosure, and configuration requirements where relevant.

Provide the full manufacturer code, including suffixes. A short family reference may identify several variants with different operating characteristics.

## Make alternatives explicit

If alternatives are acceptable, state the requirements that cannot change. We can source against a defined specification; the buyer’s engineering team must determine whether an offered alternative is appropriate for the installation.

## Quote with the documents in view

Manufacturer documentation and declarations are supplied with the part where available from the source. Include any test, conformity, or material-document requirement before quoting. We confirm what accompanies the offered item.
