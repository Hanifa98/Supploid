## Small components. Specific requirements.

Hardware, expendables, and consumables still need exact identification. Include the approved part number or standard, quantity, unit of measure, and any manufacturer restrictions in your enquiry.

For consumables, identify the required specification, packaging size, shelf-life requirement, and any storage or transport conditions. A visually similar product or matching description is not enough to establish equivalence.

## Build a usable bill of materials

One line per item makes it easier to compare availability and pricing. Email a PDF, spreadsheet, or CSV directly if you have a longer parts list. Flag items that can ship separately and those needed together for a maintenance event.

Certificates of Conformance and trace documents are supplied with the part where available from the source. Tell us about batch or lot traceability requirements before we quote.

## Quote and delivery

Fast quoting, documentation supplied. We confirm offered quantities, documentation, and delivery assumptions so your team can decide whether to accept, split, or revise the requirement.
