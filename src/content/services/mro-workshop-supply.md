## Keep the maintenance list moving

Send a single requirement or a maintenance bill of materials. Include quantity, pack size, specification, and the equipment or process involved. Flag any product restrictions and whether equivalent alternatives may be proposed.

For shelf-life-controlled or temperature-sensitive materials, specify the minimum remaining life and handling conditions your operation requires. For liquids or other controlled transport items, relevant safety and shipping documentation must be addressed with the source.

## Group the requirement sensibly

Identify which items are urgent and which can follow in a later shipment. State whether you need a consolidated delivery or can accept separate dispatches. These choices affect the usefulness of an offer as much as the unit price.

## Clear source documentation

Manufacturer CofC, material records, and applicable product declarations are supplied with the goods where available. The available documents are confirmed before quoting; Supploid passes through source documentation rather than generating manufacturer statements.

Use the industrial inquiry form, or email your part list directly. Fast quoting, documentation supplied.
